# Goblin Gazette

A paper-round minigame for World of Warcraft, part of the Nether addon family.

Fizzwick has a steam trike, a sack of papers and a street to deliver. Bright houses take the Gazette: a paper
in the mailbox scores best, the doorstep will do. Grey houses don't subscribe, and their windows are fair game.
Miss a subscriber, or break their window, and they cancel. Swerve round what's on the road, outrun the kobolds
that burst out of the yards, and pick up bundles of papers on the way. Every street ends on the Bonus Track,
and then the day's results are printed on the front page.

## Features

- Five routes, each harder than the last: the Goldshire Road in Elwynn Forest, the Sentinel Hill farms in
  Westfall, Darkshire Lane in Duskwood, the Razor Hill track in Durotar and the Booty Bay boardwalk in
  Stranglethorn. Busier roads, more kobolds and, from Westfall on, puddles (Durotar: mud) on the road.
- Mailboxes, doorsteps, windows, mailboxes to smash and yard ornaments to knock over; subscribers kept and lost.
- Hazards: obstacles, crossing chickens, oncoming horse carts, kobolds that chase you down the lane, water.
  Three trikes a round.
- The Bonus Track after every street: ramps, oil slicks, targets and dummies; hits thrown in the air score double.
- The front page: a headline written from how your round went, a photograph of the street, the round's numbers,
  a map of every house, the Bonus Track and your standing.
- The daily route: the same street for everyone in the world on the same date (UTC), with a shared leaderboard
  (today's route and best ever). Every score is checked by riding the round again before it counts.
- Bronze, silver and gold medals for each round, the best kept for each route; twelve achievements on the
  trophy shelf.
- Its own music and sound (the riding theme turns urgent when a kobold is after you), each with an on/off switch.
- Combat pauses the game at once and hides the window; nothing runs while the window is closed.

## Keys

`W` / `S` (or Up / Down): speed up / slow down. `A` / `D` (or Left / Right): swerve. `Space` or a click on the
street: throw. `Esc`: pause.

## Commands

- `/gazette` or `/goblingazette`: open or close the game
- `/gazette daily`: today's route
- `/gazette board`: the leaderboard
- `/gazette trophies`: the trophy shelf
- `/gazette sound`, `/gazette music`: sound effects and music on or off
- `/gazette size 0.5-1`: the window's size
- `/gazette perf`: frame time and memory
- `/gazette debug`: errors and missing art
- `/gazette reset`: the window's position and this character's records

Settings: Esc > Options > AddOns > Goblin Gazette (and NetherSuite's Minigames tab when it is installed).
