# Goblin Gazette changelog

## 1.0.0
- First release: ride Fizzwick's steam trike up the lane and deliver the Goblin Gazette across five routes
  (Elwynn Forest, Westfall, Duskwood, Durotar and Stranglethorn), each harder than the last, with mailboxes,
  doorsteps and the odd window; obstacles, chickens, horse carts, kobolds that chase you, and water on the road;
  the Bonus Track after every street; the round's results printed on the Gazette's front page; a daily route
  for everyone with a shared leaderboard; bronze, silver and gold medals; twelve achievements; its own music
  and sound.
